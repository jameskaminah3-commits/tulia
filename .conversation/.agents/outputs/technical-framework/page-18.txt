9. WhatsApp, notifications and number masking
PRIORITY NOTE
The WhatsApp layer is the highest-value item in the whole build. It is what reduces no-shows and drives rebooking. If schedule pressure forces a cut, cut the
therapist earnings ledger before you cut reminders.

9.2 Implementation
WhatsApp Business Cloud API. Pre-approve message templates early — approval takes days and is a common launch blocker.
Outbound messages go through a notifications queue table, dispatched by pg_cron every minute. Never send inline in a request handler; a
WhatsApp outage must not fail a booking.
Store sent_at , delivered_at , failed_at and the provider message id. Retry twice, then fall back to SMS.
Inbound webhook lands on an Edge Function. At launch, route all inbound messages to the ops console rather than attempting a bot. Conversational
booking is phase two at the earliest.
9.3 Number masking
Required before mobile (home and hotel) service goes live. Client and therapist must be able to call each other about access, parking or delays without
exchanging real numbers.
Africa's Talking voice proxy: allocate a pool of virtual numbers; bind a pair to a booking from T−2h to T+2h; route calls through and log them.
After the window closes, the binding is released and the number returns to the pool.
Call metadata (who, when, duration) is logged. Content is not recorded.
This is a genuine commercial control as well as a privacy one: it is the main technical brake on clients and therapists taking the relationship off-platform.
