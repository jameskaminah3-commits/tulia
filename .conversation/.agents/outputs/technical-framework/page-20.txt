11. Security, privacy and Kenyan obligations
11.1 What we hold
Names, phone numbers, home addresses, ID or passport numbers, payment references, appointment histories and therapist health notes. Under Kenyan
law that is a serious data set, and appointment history combined with home address is materially sensitive.
11.2 Compliance requirements
Obligation
What engineering must deliver
ODPC registration
Business action, but engineering supplies the processing description. Required where turnover exceeds
KES 5M, staff exceed 10, sensitive data is processed, or data of 10,000+ subjects is handled in a year.
Data Protection Impact Assessment
Must be completed before processing begins. Engineering documents data flows, retention and third-
party processors.
Breach notification
72 hours to the ODPC. We need audit logging good enough to establish scope quickly, and a documented
escalation path.
Consent
Freely given, specific, informed, unambiguous. Separate, unticked checkboxes for terms, marketing and
health-note storage. No pre-ticked boxes, no bundling.
Data subject rights
Export and deletion endpoints in the ops console. Deletion anonymises rather than drops booking rows,
so financial records survive.
11.3 Engineering controls
RLS on every table, verified by an automated test that fails CI if any public table has RLS disabled.
Service role key server-side only. Add a CI grep that fails the build if it appears in any client bundle.
Encrypt ID document numbers at the column level using pgcrypto , with the key in Supabase Vault. Never log them.
Private storage bucket for credential scans, accessed only through short-lived signed URLs generated server-side for ops.
Audit log table capturing every staff read of client PII, every fee waiver, every ban and every payout approval, with actor and timestamp.
Retention: GPS check-in/out points purged after 90 days; masked-call metadata after 90 days; booking and payment records retained seven years for
tax.
Secrets in Vercel and Supabase environment configuration only. Never in the repository, never in the client bundle.
Rate limiting on OTP requests and the availability RPC.
THERAPIST SAFETY DATA
Panic-button events, GPS traces and incident records exist to protect people. Access is ops-and-admin only, every access is logged, and this data is never used
for performance management. Make that explicit in the therapist-facing privacy notice — if therapists suspect the panic button is surveillance, they will not use
it, and the safety architecture fails.
