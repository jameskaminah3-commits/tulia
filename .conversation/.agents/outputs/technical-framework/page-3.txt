1. What we are building, and the one hard part
A booking, payment and dispatch platform for a premium massage service operating from a studio in Mombasa and delivering to client
homes and hotel rooms. Three user types, one database, one deployment.

1.2 The hard part
Ninety percent of this system is ordinary CRUD. One part is not: computing which therapists are genuinely available for a given treatment, time and
location, given that they move between the studio, client homes across Mombasa, and hotels along the North Coast.
A therapist finishing a session in Nyali at 14:00 cannot begin one in Shanzu at 14:15. A therapist being free does not help if both studio rooms are
occupied. Not every therapist is trained in prenatal or deep tissue. The client may have requested a specific therapist gender.
WHERE PROJECTS LIKE THIS FAIL
Double bookings and impossible travel schedules. Both destroy trust with the client and the therapist, and both are avoidable at the database layer rather than
in application code. Section 6 and 7 specify how. Please read those two sections before writing any booking logic.
1.3 Non-goals for version one
Explicitly out of scope, do not build: native iOS/Android apps, therapist self-onboarding, memberships and gift vouchers, a corporate portal, loyalty
points, multi-language, automated payout execution, review syndication to Google, or any form of surge/dynamic pricing. All are phase two. Scope creep
here will cost us the launch window.
