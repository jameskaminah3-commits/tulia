Callback functions are internet-facing and unauthenticated by design. Every one must verify a provider signature or shared secret before touching the
database, and must be safe to call twice with the same payload. Treat these as the highest-risk surface in the system.

session_watchdog is a safety control, not a housekeeping job. A therapist who has not checked out fifteen minutes past the expected end of a home
visit must produce a phone call from ops within five minutes. Build the alert path and test it.
