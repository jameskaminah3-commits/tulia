14. Definition of done
A feature ships when all of the following are true. No exceptions for launch pressure.
Schema changes are migrations in the repository, applied to staging first.
RLS policies exist and are covered by a test using a real user JWT.
Errors surface as user-readable messages, never as a raw Postgres error string.
Every write path populates bookings.origin and, for new clients, clients.acquired_via .
Anything touching money is idempotent and has a reconciliation path.
Anything touching client PII is logged in the audit table.
The flow has been walked through on a real mid-range Android phone on a mobile connection — not only on a laptop. Most of our clients and all of
our therapists will be on exactly that.
Ops can perform the equivalent action manually in the console when the automated path fails.
