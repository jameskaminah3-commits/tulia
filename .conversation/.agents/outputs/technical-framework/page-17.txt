IDEMPOTENCY IS MANDATORY
Providers retry callbacks, sometimes several times, sometimes out of order, and occasionally a callback arrives before our initiate() call has returned. The
unique (provider, provider_ref) constraint plus an upsert handles this. Never process a callback with a plain insert , and never assume a
payment row already exists when the callback lands.
8.4 Reconciliation sweep
A pg_cron job every two minutes selects payments in pending or timed_out older than 120 seconds, calls queryStatus() , and settles them. This
job is what protects us from silent money loss. Alert ops on any payment unresolved after 30 minutes.
8.5 Payouts
Weekly, Monday, covering the previous Monday to Sunday. For each therapist, sum completed bookings, apply therapists.commission_rate , add
tips, produce a payouts row in draft .
Ops approves each batch manually before any money moves. At this volume, automated disbursement is unnecessary risk. Automate later, once the
numbers have been visibly correct for three months.
Execution via provider B2C, or manually through the M-Pesa portal at launch — the console just needs to record the reference.
Therapists see their own payout history in the therapist app, itemised by booking. This is a retention feature; make it clear and never late.
